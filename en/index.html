How it works.

One example in three steps.

1. You write

Please correct the invoice for Jan Kowalski, PESEL 50810112354, account PL61 1090 1014 0000 0712 1981 2874.

No need to hide anything: the message contains a full name, a PESEL number and a bank account number.

2. The model sees

Please correct the invoice for <OSOBA_1>, PESEL <PESEL_1>, account <IBAN_1>.

Before the text reaches a model such as GPT or Claude, personal data is replaced with tokens. The AI provider gets tokens instead of the data.

3. You get back

The corrected invoice for Jan Kowalski is ready. The refund will go to account PL61 1090 1014 0000 0712 1981 2874.

The model answers with tokens, and the real data goes back in place. The token table never leaves your computer or server.

The data in this example is made up. Token names are Polish: OSOBA means person.
